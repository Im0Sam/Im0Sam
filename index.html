Sedan 2013 — The Craft

Sharper than
tradition demands

Kungliga has stood for first-class men’s grooming in Uppsala since 2013 — classic barbering built on patience, straight steel and a good conversation. Every visit starts with a consultation and ends only when the mirror agrees.

We join traditional craftsmanship with modern technique: razor-faded skin fades, scissor work shaped to how your hair actually grows, hot-towel shaves and beard architecture measured to the millimetre.
